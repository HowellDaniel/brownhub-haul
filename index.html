      How the handover works

      1. You book. A collector is rostered the night before and calls with a 2-hour window.
      2. We seal. Each piece is weighed, tagged and sealed at your door by the collector.
      3. It moves. Your bags ride a locked manifest leg. You watch the status change here.
      4. You receive. The driver hands over only against your four-digit PIN.

      Need it inside two hours? Message the depot on WhatsApp — a person answers, not a bot.

      Before you haul

      Good questions.
      Clear answers.

      Do I need an account to book?+

      No. You can book as a guest. Create an account if you want your booking history waiting whenever you log back in.

      How do I receive my bags?+

      Your confirmation contains a tracking code and a private four-digit handover PIN. Share the PIN only with the driver at handover.

      Can you deliver to a hotel instead of an airport?+

      Yes. Choose your destination in the booking form and add the hotel, room or reception reference so the collector can plan the handover.

      When do I pay?+

      Not when you book. Pay the driver or arrange WhatsApp Mobile Money when your bags are sealed at collection.
