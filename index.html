      How the handover works

      1. You book. A collector is rostered the night before and calls with a 2-hour window.
      2. We seal. Each piece is weighed, tagged and sealed at your door by the collector.
      3. It moves. Your bags ride a locked manifest leg. You watch the status change here.
      4. You receive. The driver hands over only against your four-digit PIN.
